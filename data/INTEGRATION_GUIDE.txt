WHAT CHANGES IN BEHAVIOUR
═══════════════════════════════════

Combined Score logic:
  - Each (player × unit × subtopic) combo = 1 bucket
  - Score for that bucket = AVERAGE smartScore across all attempts
  - Combined score = sum of all bucket scores
  - Retrying same subtopic just updates the average — no exploitation

Recent tab:
  - Shows raw attempts (most recent first)
  - Tap any row → opens that player's modal

Players tab:
  - One card per person ranked by combined score
  - Shows: avg acc, total correct/total, topics done, attempts
  - Progress bars for each topic they've attempted
  - Tap card → opens full drill-down modal

By Unit tab:
  - Grouped by unit
  - Players ranked within each unit by avg smartScore (not best attempt)
  - Subtopic badges showing avg acc across attempts
  - Tap any player row → opens their modal

Top Scores tab:
  - Ranked by combined score (deduped, avg-based)
  - No raw attempts — clean all-time leaderboard

Player Modal (opens on tap):
  - Bottom sheet on mobile
  - Avatar, combined score badge
  - Overview: overall acc, total correct, topics, attempts
  - Per-topic breakdown with mini trend chart (dots = accuracy over time)
  - All raw attempts listed under each topic with score/acc/pts/time
